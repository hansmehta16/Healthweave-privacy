Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

HealthWeave is currently in private development. Its wearable integrations are being tested and the service is not yet publicly launched.

1. Who we are

HealthWeave is being developed by Healthweave Limited, a company registered in the United Kingdom. Healthweave Limited is the data controller for personal information it processes through HealthWeave.

Privacy enquiries: Hans@healthweave.uk

2. Information we process

During private testing, HealthWeave may process account information, authentication identifiers, technical information needed to operate the service, and information voluntarily supplied by testers.

HealthWeave is developing integrations with wearable services, including WHOOP. Once a tester explicitly authorises a WHOOP connection, the integration is intended to retrieve permitted categories of data, which may include:

  • Sleep duration and sleep-related measurements
  • Recovery scores, heart rate variability and resting heart rate
  • Daily activity, strain and cardiovascular measurements
  • Workout and exercise information
  • Body measurements, where authorised

The exact information available depends on the permissions granted and the data supplied by WHOOP. A WHOOP connection is not yet operational.

3. How we use information

We use information to develop, operate, secure and test HealthWeave, authenticate accounts, troubleshoot technical problems and, when integrations become operational, display authorised wearable information.

We do not currently operate integrations that disclose wearable health data to insurers or healthcare providers. Any future sharing features would require separate privacy information and appropriate user controls and legal safeguards.

4. Legal bases for processing

Depending on the activity, we may rely on contractual necessity, legitimate interests or consent under applicable UK data protection law.

Wearable health information may constitute special category personal data. Before processing such data, we must establish both a lawful basis under Article 6 and an applicable condition under Article 9 of the UK GDPR. Where we rely on explicit consent, we will request it separately and provide a means to withdraw it.

5. Storage and security

HealthWeave uses Supabase for backend services, including authentication and database functionality. The WHOOP integration has been designed to encrypt wearable access credentials before database storage.

We use access controls and other technical safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Before wider testing, we will review hosting locations, international data transfers and the safeguards applicable to our service providers.

6. Sharing information

We may use service providers, including Supabase, to provide infrastructure necessary to operate HealthWeave. Such providers may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual arrangements.

HealthWeave does not currently sell wearable health data or provide it to insurers or doctors through the proposed future sharing features.

We may also disclose information where legally required or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

7. Retention and deletion

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to legal obligations and legitimate requirements. Retention and deletion procedures are being finalised during private development.

To request deletion of personal information or withdrawal of an applicable consent, contact Hans@healthweave.uk .

Disconnecting a wearable connection does not necessarily delete information previously imported. Deletion requests will be assessed and handled under applicable data protection law.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict their personal information, object to certain processing, and request data portability.

Where processing relies on consent, that consent may be withdrawn. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

You can contact us using the email above. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as HealthWeave develops, particularly when new integrations or data-sharing features become available. Material changes will be communicated where required.
